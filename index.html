From shortlist to shipment

Compare OssBuy finds by fit

For sneaker sizing, measure both feet and check the seller’s size chart against your longer foot. Compare the listed insole length when available. For hoodies, measure chest width, sleeve length and body length on a garment you already wear. Check whether the chart gives flat measurements or circumference.

Verify OssBuy product links

Open the original seller listing and match the listing ID, colorway, size option and product photos. Save the selected variant with the seller link. If the item redirects to another listing or the photos change, check the variant again before ordering.

Review OssBuy QC photos

Use warehouse inspection photos to check the shoe size label, outsole and both sides of the pair. On clothing, look at seams, zippers and the garment tag. For accessories, ask for a close-up of the clasp or hardware when the detail is unclear.

Compare OssBuy shipping estimates

Use your destination and postal code to compare available shipping routes, tracking and delivery windows. Record the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, currency conversion and estimated international freight. Check applicable import charges for your destination and compare actual versus volumetric weight before approving the parcel.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OssBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that organizes product finds by category. This guide points to Hipobuyfind category pages, where you can browse listings and check the original seller details.

What should I check in QC photos?

Compare the received item's size label, color and visible condition with the seller listing. Ask for close-ups of any marks or missing details before approving shipment.

Can I estimate shipping before ordering?

Yes. Use the destination country and postal code, then compare estimates using packed weight and box dimensions. The final charge depends on the parcel and shipping route.

Does a reference photo confirm stock or authenticity?

A reference photo illustrates the style. Check stock with the seller and seek independent authentication when authenticity matters.
